Suggested for comedies such as Konosuba. Sedita's types include the Logical Smart One, the Lovable Loser, the Neurotic, the Dimwit, the Narcissist, the Materialist and the Womanizer or Manizer (unverified, from memory). A comic character rarely learns; the fixed flaw is the engine. Tends to explain a story best when characters stay flat and the plot comes from their flaws meeting.
