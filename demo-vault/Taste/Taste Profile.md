# Taste Profile

What the demo writer likes in stories, learned from his keep and drop decisions on the Ideas board. His words outrank inference.

## Loves
- Props you can see and hold: the Warden's ledger, the cracked bell hidden with wax. ← I-006, I-014 · medium
- Kindness that costs something real. ← I-015 · medium
- Small tells in a character's body: Mara humming when she lies. ← I-010 · low

## Avoids
- Twists that undo a death. ← I-009 (Cliché, Too clever) · medium
- Grim turns in a cozy book. ← I-003, I-013 (Wrong tone) · medium

## Open questions
- Does a second bellmaker fail because we already have one rival, or because rivals are not wanted at all?
